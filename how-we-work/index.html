Working with Qualitas

Clear scope, fixed cadence and accountable deliverables.

Every engagement is different, but the way we work is consistent.

01

Start with the pressure point

A short conversation establishes the project stage, concern and decision. We then agree scope, deliverables and timeline in writing.

02

Set the cadence

Each deliverable has an owner, a due date and an acceptance step. A regular working cycle is consolidated into periodic formal reporting.

03

Keep governance clear

We coordinate through a single client contact, document communication and escalate material cost, programme, quality or risk issues promptly.

04

Protect independence

We remain separate from the parties whose work we measure because that separation makes our output credible to owners, lenders and auditors.

05

Transfer the output

Deliverables and project data transfer to the client on payment. We protect confidential information and retain only our background methods and tools.

Engagement rhythm

Consistency catches risk while it is still small.

After mobilisation we establish the working basis and data interfaces, then settle into a fixed cycle. Toward completion the focus moves to closing the record and handing over outputs and intellectual property.

Fees

Scoped and priced in writing.

Engagements are typically priced on a man-month or deliverable basis, so commitments are clear before work begins.
