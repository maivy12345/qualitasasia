Privacy Policy

How Qualitas Engineering Asia Limited collects, uses and protects personal data.

PrivacyTermsCookiesDisclaimer

Qualitas Engineering Asia Limited (“we”, “us”, “our”) is committed to protecting your personal data and complying with the Hong Kong Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486) (PDPO).

Personal data we collect

We collect information you provide directly, including your name, company, email address, telephone number and message when you contact us. We may also collect limited technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and time of access through cookies and analytics.

Purposes

We use personal data to respond to enquiries, provide and improve services, operate and secure the website, send requested information and comply with legal obligations.

Disclosure

We do not sell personal data. We may share it with service providers supporting hosting, email and analytics under appropriate confidentiality and data-protection terms, or with authorities where required by law.

Transfer, storage and retention

Data may be processed in Hong Kong or other jurisdictions with appropriate safeguards. Enquiry records are retained for up to 24 months after last contact unless a relationship continues; analytics data follows provider settings, typically 14 months; legally required records are kept as long as required.

Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures, including access controls, encryption in transit and secure hosting.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data, or withdraw consent. Contact contact@qualitasasia.com.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will remain available on this page.
