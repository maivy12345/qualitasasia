Corrective action

Non-conformances are a sign of a healthy system, not a failure

A working quality system finds problems, understands them and proves they were closed.

A project with zero recorded non-conformances is not usually a sign of perfect quality. It is often a sign that problems are going unrecorded.

The discipline is in closure. An NCR is raised, the issue dispositioned — use-as-is, rework, repair or reject — root-caused, and a Corrective Action verified effective before closure.

The register is the memory of the system

Trending matters as much as closing individual items. A recurring non-conformance points to a systemic cause that a single rework will not fix.

We treat the NCR/CAR register as evidence of a working system: problems found, understood and genuinely closed.
