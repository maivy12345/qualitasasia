Compliance review

Desktop compliance review: independence without the site visit

A documented second line of assurance over digital project records.

Modern projects generate quality records digitally — material certificates, compaction results, concrete cube strengths and test reports — as the work proceeds.

A desktop review samples these records against the specification: were the right tests done, at the right frequency, by an accredited laboratory, with results meeting the acceptance criteria?

Independent, but not site supervision

Desktop review is not a replacement for statutory site supervision, which remains with licensed local parties. It is an independent second line — a check that the quality evidence is complete and consistent.

The value is an objective, auditable confirmation that the records support the works without adding bodies to a crowded site.
