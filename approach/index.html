Control principles

How we work.

01

Independent judgement

Maintain the distance needed to make objective technical assessments.

02

Risk-led effort

Concentrate review and witnessing on high-consequence systems, interfaces and decisions.

03

Traceable evidence

Link each conclusion to a requirement, record, owner and closure status.

04

Operations in the room

Use operator knowledge to shape requirements, tests, training and acceptance.

Governance cadence

A reporting rhythm built for decisions.

Meetings and reports should resolve risk, not reproduce information already available elsewhere.

Working control

Short-cycle coordination of readiness, interfaces, evidence and blockers.

Stage assurance

Structured review against criteria before consequential project decisions.

Executive visibility

Concise reporting of exposure, trend, ownership and required action.

Issue management

Close the cause, not only the comment.

Issues are classified by technical and operational consequence, assigned to accountable owners and closed only when the evidence supports the outcome.
