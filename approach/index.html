Our approach

Plan, review, report, correct and hand over.

Qualitas runs a quality-management cycle to ISO 9001: plan the system, review records independently, drive non-conformances to closure and assemble the evidence.

A repeatable quality cycle

From intent to verified evidence.

Controls become useful when every decision connects to evidence. Each step strengthens the next, creating a working record that stays current from mobilisation to handover.

Working principles

The discipline behind every decision.

Five habits connect the work front to an evidence trail that is clear, current and ready for scrutiny.

05working
habits
01

Plan before work

A quality plan written after the work is a record, not a control. We agree criteria and hold points first.

↗
02

Sample by risk

Review effort is weighted toward the work types where defects matter most, not the records easiest to open.

↗
03

Report for action

Findings are prioritised by severity and issued promptly so the team can act while options remain.

↗
04

Verify effectiveness

Non-conformances close only after the corrective action is shown to work.

↗
05

Compile progressively

Handover becomes a completeness check, not a reconstruction from scattered records.

↗

How we assure our work

Senior review, version control and a clear audit trail.

Where independence is required, the person who reviews is not the person who produced. Our output is traceable to who produced it, when and against which source record.
