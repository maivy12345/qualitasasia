Service 05

ESG monitoring & reporting frameworks

We set up the framework to monitor and report ESG on the project record.

The challenge

ESG performance must be evidenced, not asserted.

Without a framework to capture performance on the project record, ESG becomes a claim that cannot survive due diligence.

Our approach

Align criteria, evidence and reporting.

We establish the monitoring structure, integrate evidence into project documentation, report periodically and identify gaps.

What we do

  • Agree material ESG criteria and reporting boundaries
  • Map each criterion to evidence, frequency and owner
  • Integrate evidence capture into project controls
  • Review gaps and actions on the reporting cycle
  • Support investor and green-finance due diligence

Standards and engagement

Standards: Recognised ESG reporting frameworks.

Typical engagement: Framework set up early with reporting on the project cycle.

Key deliverables

  • ESG monitoring and reporting framework
  • Criteria, evidence and ownership register
  • Periodic ESG report
  • Gap and action tracker

Who it’s for

Developers, investors, lenders and asset owners that need evidenced ESG reporting from financing or mobilisation through delivery and operation.

Outcome

ESG performance that is structured, evidenced and ready for investor reporting.
