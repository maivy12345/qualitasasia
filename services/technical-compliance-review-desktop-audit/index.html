Service 02

Technical-compliance review (desktop audit)

We check, independently, that the quality records actually add up.

The challenge

Digital records create a powerful second line of assurance.

Material certificates, compaction results and cube strengths can be sampled independently to verify whether the record supports the works.

Our approach

Review frequency, provenance, acceptance and trend.

We check whether the right tests were done at the right frequency, by an accredited laboratory where required, with results meeting the criteria. This remains distinct from statutory site supervision.

What we do

  • Set a risk-based sampling plan for digital project records
  • Check test frequency, provenance and acceptance criteria
  • Confirm accredited-laboratory status where required
  • Analyse trends and identify emerging drift
  • Escalate material exceptions on the agreed reporting cycle

Standards and engagement

Standards: ISO 9001 and ISO/IEC 17025 awareness for laboratory results.

Typical engagement: Reviews on a fixed cycle, sized to the project’s record volume.

Key deliverables

  • Desktop Compliance Report
  • Sample and exception register
  • Test-frequency and acceptance review
  • Trend summary with prioritised actions

Who it’s for

Developers, investors, lenders and asset owners that already receive digital quality records and need independent second-line assurance during construction or due diligence.

Outcome

An independent, documented confirmation that the records support the works — or a clear flag where they do not.
