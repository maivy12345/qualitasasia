Service 04

Commissioning & handover dossier governance

We assemble the evidence that the project is complete and acceptable.

The challenge

The dossier is the proof of what was built.

An incomplete or inconsistent record stalls due diligence and erodes value when the project changes hands.

Our approach

Define the index first and compile by package.

We structure the commissioning and as-built index, compile evidence as work proceeds, review completeness and manage defects to sign-off.

What we do

  • Commissioning and as-built documentation index
  • Completeness and consistency review
  • Quality dossier compilation by work package
  • Snagging and defects management to sign-off
  • Handover-readiness assessment for investors

Standards and engagement

Standards: Investor-grade documentation and specification compliance.

Typical engagement: Assembled progressively and finalised at completion.

Outcome

A complete, consistent dossier that stands up to investor and auditor due diligence.

Make handover a verification, not a reconstruction.

Start the dossier structure while the evidence can still be captured.
