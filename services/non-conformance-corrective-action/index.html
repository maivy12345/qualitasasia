Service 03

Non-conformance & corrective action

We make sure problems are recorded, root-caused and genuinely closed.

The challenge

Zero recorded NCRs rarely means perfect quality.

A healthy system surfaces non-conformances because surfacing them is how they get fixed.

Our approach

Close only when corrective action is verified effective.

Each NCR is dispositioned, root-caused and tracked. The register is trended to expose systemic causes that a single rework will not fix.

What we do

  • Raise, track and close NCRs
  • Disposition and root-cause analysis
  • Corrective Action Requests and effectiveness verification
  • NCR/CAR register maintenance and trending
  • Escalation of systemic quality issues

Standards and engagement

Standards: ISO 9001 corrective-action discipline.

Typical engagement: Continuous through construction as part of the assurance role.

Outcome

Quality problems are surfaced and resolved, not buried — with a register that proves it.

Close the cause, not only the comment.

Bring discipline and visibility to your NCR/CAR process.
