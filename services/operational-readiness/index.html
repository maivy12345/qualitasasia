Move from project completion to controlled operation.

Align people, information, procedures and asset status so the operating team can take ownership with clarity.

The need

Handover is an operating decision, not a document transfer.

Operational readiness brings asset information, procedures, training, spares and residual risk into one controlled transition plan.

O&M readinessTrainingAsset dataSOP / MOP / EOPGo-live
Scope

Readiness across people, process and plant.

The operating model is assessed alongside physical completion and commissioning status.

01

Readiness criteria

Define the minimum evidence and conditions for energisation, occupancy or go-live.

02

Information readiness

Review O&M manuals, asset data, as-builts, certificates and systems information.

03

Procedure readiness

Coordinate operating, maintenance and emergency procedures with verified system behaviour.

04

People readiness

Structure training, competency evidence, familiarisation and role ownership.

05

Transition control

Track defects, access, permits, warranties, spares and residual risk through handover.

Readiness gate

A clear basis for the go-live decision.

Decision-makers see what is complete, what remains, who owns it and whether the residual risk is acceptable.

Physical state

Installation, labelling, access, safety and defects.

Performance state

Testing, integrated response, exceptions and retest.

Operating state

People, procedures, records, tools and response capability.

Typical outputs

Transition evidence that operations can use.

Readiness outputs are practical, owned and linked to the final acceptance decision.

A

Readiness matrix

Criteria, evidence, status, owner and risk across each operating domain.

B

Training and procedure register

Required capabilities, approved procedures and completion evidence.

C

Go-live recommendation

A concise position on readiness, exceptions, controls and residual risk.
