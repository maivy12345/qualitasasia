The need

When to engage

Use governance before complexity becomes ambiguity.

  • Owner requirements are incomplete or distributed across documents.
  • Design packages depend on multiple vendors or disciplines.
  • Frequent changes are affecting testing, programme or compliance.
  • Technical decisions lack clear authority or closure evidence.
  • The project needs an independent position before a stage gate.
