Service 01

Quality management frameworks & ITPs

We build the quality system the site team applies, so quality is planned, not improvised.

The challenge

Quality must be planned before defects become hidden.

You cannot inspect quality into a finished structure. By the time a defect is visible at the end, it is buried, expensive and often unfixable.

Our approach

Define the control before the work.

We prepare the Project Quality Plan and work-type ITPs against the specification, defining acceptance criteria, methods, frequencies and the hold and witness points where work must stop for inspection.

What we do

  • Project Quality Plan and work-type ITPs
  • Hold points, witness points and acceptance criteria
  • Control forms and checklists
  • Material-approval procedures and source qualification
  • Guidance for the client’s on-site team

Standards and engagement

Standards: ISO 9001, project specification and local standards.

Typical engagement: Established at mobilisation and updated as work types come on stream.

Outcome

A clear, specification-aligned quality system that defines what good looks like before work starts.
