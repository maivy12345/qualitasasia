Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about our assurance role.

Scope, standards, engagement structure, reporting and ownership.

Do you perform site supervision or in-situ acceptance?

No. We provide quality assurance and desktop review of records. Statutory site supervision and acceptance remain with licensed local parties.

What standards do you work to?

ISO 9001 for quality management and ISO/IEC 17025 awareness for laboratory results, against the project specification and applicable local standards.

What is a desktop compliance review?

An independent, sampling-based review of quality and test records on the cloud, checked against the specification and issued as a Desktop Compliance Report.

How do you handle quality problems?

Through NCRs that are dispositioned, root-caused and closed only after a Corrective Action is verified effective.

Can you prepare a handover dossier for investors?

Yes. We structure and review the commissioning and as-built dossier to an investor-ready standard.

Do you provide ESG reporting?

Yes. We establish monitoring and reporting frameworks and integrate evidence into the project record.

Where is the work performed?

Reviews are desktop reviews performed from our Hong Kong office over digital records.

Who owns the deliverables?

The client does. Frameworks, reports and project data transfer to the client on payment.

Where are you based?

7A/F, Wing Hing Commercial Building, 139 Wing Lok St, Sheung Wan, Hong Kong.

How do we start working with you?

Contact us by email or through the contact page. We will arrange a short call to understand the project and propose a way forward.

Do you work internationally or remotely?

Yes. Most work is delivered through digital collaboration, with clear deliverables and a defined reporting cadence.

How are engagements structured and priced?

Each engagement has a written scope, deliverables and fee, typically on a man-month or deliverable basis.

Can you join a project already underway?

Yes. We mobilise on live projects, establish a working basis quickly and refine from there without stopping the works.

How quickly can you mobilise?

Usually within the first reporting cycle, prioritising a credible working basis and data feed before refining detail.

Who will do the work?

Senior specialists. We are deliberately a small, hands-on team.

How do you ensure output is reliable?

Senior review, version control, recognised standards and, where required, separation of reviewer and producer.

What do you need from us to start?

A clear point of contact, access to relevant information and agreement on scope and cadence. Data interfaces are defined up front.

Do you sign non-disclosure agreements?

Yes. We can work under your NDA or ours, and treat project information as confidential by default.

How do you report and communicate?

Through one point of contact on a fixed cadence: a regular working cycle plus a periodic formal report, with prompt escalation of material issues.

What deliverables will we receive?

The agreed scope identifies each client-owned output before work starts. Depending on the service, this may include quality plans and ITPs, compliance reports, NCR/CAR registers, handover-readiness records or ESG reports.
