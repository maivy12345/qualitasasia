About Qualitas

Evidence that the works meet the specification.

A Hong Kong-based technical governance and quality-assurance consultancy supporting infrastructure projects across Asia.

Our role

Independent, disciplined and clearly bounded.

We support infrastructure projects with quality-management frameworks, technical-compliance review, commissioning-documentation governance and ESG reporting.

We work to ISO 9001 and the principles of ISO/IEC 17025 for laboratory results, against the project specification and applicable local standards. Our reviews are desktop reviews of digital records: inspection and test plans, material approvals, test results and non-conformance closure.

We provide quality assurance and review on records — not statutory site supervision. We do not perform in-situ acceptance or sign site records governed by local construction-supervision law; those remain with the licensed local parties.

Independent review. Traceable evidence. Clear boundaries.
